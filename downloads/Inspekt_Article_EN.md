# Façade inspection: locating, documenting and assessing anomalies

A close-up photograph can show an anomaly on a façade. Before deciding what to do, the technical team needs to know where it is, when the image was captured and what further checks may be needed.

The Inspekt AI platform brings inspection images and information about detected anomalies together, linking them to their location on the building. This helps professionals identify the area to review and consult the associated records. Classification and filters make the information easier to navigate, while artificial intelligence supports analysis and specialist review. [Explore the platform](https://inspektai.com/defect-analysis-dashboard/).

Earlier inspections add an essential dimension: time. Comparing photographs of the same area taken on different dates helps teams assess how an anomaly has evolved, provided the images are suitable for comparison. Camera angle, lighting and image quality can affect how changes appear. Where suitable earlier records are unavailable, the first inspection establishes a baseline for future monitoring. Inspekt AI provides this [inspection comparison capability](https://inspektai.com/year-on-year-comparison/).

Thermal imaging can complement a visual inspection when the objectives of the work call for it. Interpretation requires suitable capture conditions and specialist knowledge. Relating a colour to a temperature requires the scale and measurement settings; an image alone cannot establish the cause of an anomaly or provide a structural diagnosis.

For those responsible for building maintenance, having this information organised helps with planning work and setting priorities. The responsible technical professionals assess whether site visits, testing or other checks are needed before deciding on an intervention.

If you are planning work on a façade, start by sharing the building's location, what you need to assess and your intended timing. With this information, the Inspekt AI team can advise on the inspection scope.
